About

I hold a B.Sc. in Engineering Science from the University of Tehran. My bachelor’s thesis studied inverse design of analog filters using machine learning, with a focus on Sallen–Key circuits.

My engineering work includes embedded systems, robotic control, and sensor integration. This portfolio brings together my thesis, research-related projects, education, and published work on gait sensing.
